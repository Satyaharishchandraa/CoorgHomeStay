About Us

The story behind the homestay.

A private homestay in Uluguli village near Suntikoppa, designed for families and groups looking to stay together while exploring Coorg.

Our Home in Uluguli

Set among the coffee estates of Kodagu.

The Coorg Chimm's Camptime Homestay is located in Uluguli village, near Suntikoppa, in the heart of Coorg's coffee-growing country. The surrounding land carries the greenery and quiet that this part of Kodagu is known for.

The entire property includes three bedrooms, two washrooms and one kitchen, with accommodation for a minimum of 4 and a maximum of 12 guests.

Hospitality, Homestay-Style

A smaller, more personal way to stay in Coorg.

The property is offered as one complete stay, giving families and groups a shared base with three bedrooms, two washrooms and a kitchen. Its setting in Uluguli places guests close to the greenery and coffee-country landscape of Coorg.

For booking details, send your preferred dates and guest count and the property will confirm availability and rates directly.
