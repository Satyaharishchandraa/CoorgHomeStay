Things to Do in Coorg

Waterfalls, viewpoints and coffee country around Kodagu.

Coorg (Kodagu) is spread across hills, forests and coffee estates, so most visitors base themselves in one place and take day trips out. Here's what's within reach from a stay near Suntikoppa.

[ADD PHOTO: Greenway Coffee Island]

Greenway Coffee Island Plantation Tour

A working coffee plantation offering a closer look at how Coorg's coffee is grown and processed.

[ADD PHOTO: Raja's Tomb / Gaddige]

Raja's Tomb (Gaddige)

The domed tombs of the Kodagu royal family, near Madikeri.

[ADD PHOTO: Raja Seat]

Raja Seat

A well-known viewpoint in Madikeri overlooking the surrounding valleys — a good spot for sunset.

Distance from homestay: [ADD VERIFIED DISTANCE]

[ADD PHOTO: Madikeri Fort]

Madikeri Fort

A historic fort in the middle of Madikeri town, with a museum and temple inside its grounds.

[ADD PHOTO: Abbi Falls]

Abbi Falls

A waterfall set inside a coffee and spice plantation, reached via a short walk from the car park.

[ADD PHOTO: Harangi Reservoir]

Harangi Reservoir

A dam and reservoir with garden areas, a popular short outing in the region.

[ADD PHOTO: Chiklihole Reservoir]

Chiklihole Reservoir

A quieter reservoir surrounded by greenery, known for its still water and hillside views.

[ADD PHOTO: Nisargadhama]

Nisargadhama

A forested island park on the Cauvery river, reached by a hanging bridge.

[ADD PHOTO: Dubare Elephant Camp]

Dubare Elephant Camp

A riverside camp on the Cauvery where captive elephants are cared for and, at times, visitors can observe or take part in feeding and bathing sessions.

Namdroling Monastery (Golden Temple)

A large Tibetan Buddhist monastery near Kushalnagar, known for its striking golden statues.

Mandalpatti Peak

A hilltop viewpoint above the clouds, usually reached by jeep from the base.
