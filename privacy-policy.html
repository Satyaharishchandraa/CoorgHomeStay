Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Drafted for the current static website setup. The property owner should review these terms before the site goes live.

Last updated: 27 August 2026.

This is a general website privacy draft for The Coorg Chimm's Camptime Homestay. The property owner should review it before publication and update it if the website later adds analytics, online payments, booking software, cookies or other data-processing services.

Information you provide

The website includes a booking enquiry form. The current static implementation validates the information in your browser and prepares a WhatsApp message; it does not currently send the form submission to a website database or server.

If you contact the property by phone or WhatsApp, the information you choose to share may include your name, phone number, travel dates, guest count and other booking details.

How information is used

Information shared directly with the property is used to respond to enquiries, discuss availability and rates, arrange stays and communicate about the booking.

Cookies and analytics

The current website does not include Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or another analytics service in its code, and no advertising cookies are intentionally set by the website itself. Google Maps, WhatsApp and other third-party services may apply their own technical or privacy practices when you use them.

Third-party services

The website may link to or embed services such as Google Maps and WhatsApp. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Review the relevant provider's policy when using those services.

Data retention

Because the current enquiry form does not submit information to a website database, any booking information retained by the property is handled through the communication channel used to contact the property. The owner should establish and document a specific retention period if customer records are stored.

Your requests

If you want to ask about information shared with the property or request correction or deletion, contact the property using the phone number or WhatsApp option on the Contact page.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through WhatsApp or by calling .

This page is a general draft and should be reviewed against the property's actual data practices and applicable law before publication.
