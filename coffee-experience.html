Coorg Coffee Experience

Coffee country, up close.

Coffee is at the centre of Kodagu's landscape and economy — here's what makes it worth exploring during your stay.

Why Coorg grows coffee

Kodagu's elevation, rainfall and forest cover make it one of India's established coffee-growing districts, producing both Arabica and Robusta beans, typically grown in the shade of larger trees alongside pepper vines and other spices.

What a plantation visit looks like

Plantation tours generally walk visitors through how coffee is grown, harvested and processed, from the shaded rows of coffee plants to drying and roasting. Greenway Coffee Island, near the homestay, is one option for this kind of tour — see things to do in Coorg for more on visiting it.

Coffee at the homestay

Because the property sits within Uluguli's coffee-growing landscape, the estates are part of the everyday view here rather than a separate excursion — mornings often begin with mist settling over the coffee rows nearby.

Bringing coffee home
