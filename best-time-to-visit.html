Best Time to Visit Coorg

Choosing your season in Kodagu.

Coorg looks and feels different across the year — here's what to expect in each season.

Winter, October to February

Generally considered the most comfortable season, with cool mornings, clear skies and good visibility at viewpoints. This is peak season for sightseeing, so popular spots and roads can get busy, especially around holidays.

Summer, March to May

Warmer and drier than winter, though still cooler than the plains of Karnataka. Coffee plants typically flower after the first pre-monsoon showers around this time, briefly covering the estates in white blossom.

Monsoon, June to September

Coorg receives heavy rainfall in the monsoon months, and the hills turn a deep, saturated green with waterfalls at their fullest. Some roads and trails can be affected by rain, so this season suits travellers who don't mind wet weather and want to see the region at its lushest.

Our take for a homestay stay

Because Uluguli sits inside coffee-estate country, the property looks good across seasons — misty in winter, in bloom in early summer, and deep green through the monsoon. Let us know your travel window when you send a booking enquiry and we can share what to expect at that time of year.
