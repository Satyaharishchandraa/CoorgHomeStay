Getting Here

Travelling to Uluguli

By Road

The homestay is near Suntikoppa and Google currently estimates about 25 minutes by car to Raja’s Seat in Madikeri; allow extra time for traffic and hill roads.

Nearest Town

Suntikoppa is the nearest local town. The property is in Uluguli Village, near Suntikoppa.

Nearest Rail/Air

Kannur International Airport is the nearest major airport listed for the area; Mysuru and Mangaluru are other practical options. See our How to Reach Coorg guide.
