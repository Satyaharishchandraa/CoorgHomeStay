The Entire Homestay

Three bedrooms. One private stay in Coorg.

The Coorg Chimm’s Camptime Homestay is offered as one complete property, giving families and groups private use of 3 bedrooms, 2 washrooms and 1 kitchen for 4 to 12 guests.

Stay together, under one roof.
