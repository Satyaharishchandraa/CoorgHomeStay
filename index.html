Experience Coorg

Coffee country, at a slower pace.

Kodagu is known for its rolling coffee estates, thick greenery and cool hill air, and Uluguli sits right inside that landscape. Mornings here tend to start slow — mist over the plantations, birdsong, the smell of coffee — before the day opens up toward Coorg's forts, falls, temples and viewpoints.

Staying at a homestay rather than a large resort means a smaller, more personal base to return to after a day of sightseeing — close enough to Suntikoppa and Madikeri for day trips, but quiet enough to actually unwind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before you book.

Where is The Coorg Chimm's Camptime Homestay located?

The homestay is located at Odiyappana House, Post, Suntikoppa, Gaddehalla, Uluguli Village, Uluguli, Karnataka 571237, India.

How many guests can stay?

The entire property is available for a minimum of 4 guests and a maximum of 12 guests.

Is the property rented room by room?

No. The entire homestay is offered as one property; the stay includes 3 bedrooms, 2 washrooms and 1 kitchen.

How do I enquire about dates and rates?

Send your preferred dates and guest count through the booking enquiry form or WhatsApp, and the property will confirm availability and rates directly.

How far is the homestay from Madikeri?

Google currently estimates about 25 minutes by car to Raja’s Seat in Madikeri; actual travel time varies with traffic and road conditions.

What attractions can I visit near the homestay?

Nearby attractions include Raja Seat, Madikeri Fort, Abbi Falls, Mandalpatti, Dubare Elephant Camp, Nisargadhama, the Golden Temple and coffee plantations around Coorg. See our full things-to-do guide.

How can I book a stay?

You can book directly by sending a booking enquiry, messaging us on WhatsApp, or calling .
