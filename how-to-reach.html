How to Reach Coorg

Getting to Kodagu, by road, rail or air.

Coorg district has no airport or railway station of its own, so most journeys combine a flight or train to a nearby city with a road transfer.

By air

Kannur International Airport (CNN) is the nearest major airport to the Suntikoppa area. Google currently estimates about 2 hr 49 min by car from the homestay to Kannur International Airport. Mysuru and Mangaluru airports are other practical options; Bengaluru offers more connections but involves a longer road transfer. Travel time varies with traffic and weather.

By rail

Coorg has no railway station of its own. Coorg has no railway station of its own. Mysuru Junction and Mangaluru-area stations are commonly used railheads, followed by a road journey into Kodagu.

By road

Coorg is well connected by road from Bengaluru, Mysuru and Mangaluru, with state and private buses, taxis and self-drive all common options. The roads climb steadily into the hills as you approach the district, with the final stretch typically the most scenic — and the slowest.

Reaching the homestay

The Coorg Chimm's Camptime Homestay is at Gaddehalla, Uluguli Village, Suntikoppa, Kodagu, Karnataka 571237. See our location page for the Google Maps link and directions once you're in the Coorg region.
