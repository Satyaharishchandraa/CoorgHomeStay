10 best places to visit in Coorg.

A condensed list of the region's most useful day-trip destinations, ordered roughly by distance from the homestay near Suntikoppa. See our full Things to Do guide for photos and Google Maps links to each one.

  1. Greenway Coffee Island Plantation Tour — a nearby coffee and nature experience. Approx. 11 min drive.
  2. Kaveri Nisargadhama — a forested river island on the Cauvery, reached by a bridge. Approx. 22 min drive.
  3. Chiklihole Reservoir — a quieter reservoir and dam surrounded by greenery. Approx. 24 min drive.
  4. Raja's Seat — a well-known Madikeri viewpoint over the surrounding valleys. Approx. 25 min drive.
  5. Madikeri Fort — a historic fort and palace complex with a museum. Approx. 25 min drive.
  6. Harangi Reservoir / Backwaters — a scenic reservoir and dam area. Approx. 31–32 min drive.
  7. Abbey Falls — a popular waterfall surrounded by coffee estates near Madikeri. Approx. 33 min drive.
  8. Dubare Elephant Camp — a riverside wildlife experience on the Cauvery. Approx. 35 min drive.
  9. Namdroling Monastery / Golden Temple — a striking Tibetan Buddhist monastery in Bylakuppe. Approx. 36 min drive.
  10. Mandalpatti Peak — a high viewpoint known for sweeping hill views and 4x4 routes. Approx. 1 hr 20 min drive.

Driving times are approximate and will vary with traffic and road conditions. For photos, descriptions and a direct "View on Google Maps" button for each stop, see the full Things to Do in Coorg guide.
