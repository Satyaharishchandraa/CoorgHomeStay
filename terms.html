Enquiries and bookings

The website is an enquiry channel and does not provide instant online booking. Guests should send their preferred dates and guest count through the enquiry form, WhatsApp or phone. The property is offered as one complete stay rather than as individually bookable bedrooms. The property includes 3 bedrooms, 2 washrooms and 1 kitchen and is intended for a minimum of 4 and maximum of 12 guests.
